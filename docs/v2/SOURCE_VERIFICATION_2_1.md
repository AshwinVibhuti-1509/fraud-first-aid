# Source verification and scope — 2026-11-01

Documentation here records *located* primary-source pages; it does not mean a lawyer/bank fraud officer independently approved the interpretation. Obtain independent review before real financial guidance is offered.

- National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal: https://cybercrime.gov.in/ — official reporting for cybercrime; page prominently gives 1930 for immediate financial cyberfraud reporting. Do not require this app before official reporting.
- RBI, July 6, 2017: https://rbi.org.in/commonman/Upload/English/Notification/PDFs/NOTI1506072017.PDF — customer protection for specified *unauthorised electronic banking transactions*. It has differing bank/customer/third-party responsibility rules and conditional reporting timelines. **It cannot be generalized to every user-authorized payment induced by deception.** This release has no automated calculation.
- RBI public-awareness guidance: https://www.rbi.org.in/commonman/english/Scripts/SMSLimitedliability.aspx — bank contact and acknowledgment guidance.
- DoT Sanchar Saathi: https://www.sancharsaathi.gov.in/ — suspected fraud communications, checking connections and telecom-related services. **Chakshu is not an alternative channel for actual financial cybercrime reporting.**
- SBI official contact centre: https://sbi.co.in/web/customer-care/contact-centre — `1800 1111 09` listed for unauthorized transaction reporting; check current listing before use.
- Bank contact reference links already in the classic project: https://www.hdfc.bank.in/contact-us, https://www.icici.bank.in/customer-care, https://www.axisbank.com/contact-us. Displayed contact numbers were drawn from earlier project records and must be rechecked by the user against the bank's genuine website; avoid relying on any unsolicited number.
- Delhi Police cyber cell: https://cyber.delhipolice.gov.in/vishing.html — general phishing/vishing and credential-exposure guidance.

Additional bank support workflows and RBI grievance escalation remain outside tested content, and exact current online portal form requirements may change. Prior archived foreign references remain in the data dictionary for historical provenance; their legal remedies are not applied to Indian customers.
